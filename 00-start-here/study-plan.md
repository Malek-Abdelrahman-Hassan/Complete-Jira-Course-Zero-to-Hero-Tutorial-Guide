## Study loop for each lesson

1. **Preview (2 min).** Read the lesson note's TL;DR and key concepts, and predict what the video will show.
2. **Watch actively.** Pause to reproduce every step in your own Jira sandbox site instead of only watching.
3. **Compare with the note.** Read the rest of the note. Write down anything the video made clearer, or anything the note misses, in your own words.
4. **Practice.** Do the matching exercise from the section's *Hands-on practice* list.
5. **Self-check.** Close the video and answer the review questions from memory, then check the answers.
6. **Close the loop.** Log the session in your private study log and tick off the lesson.

## Definition of done for a lesson

- [ ] The TL;DR can be restated from memory.
- [ ] Every Jira action from the lesson has been tried in a sandbox site.
- [ ] Every review question can be answered without looking.
- [ ] Anything the video showed that the note misses has been added, or suggested as a fix.
- [ ] The session is logged.

## Review schedule

Spaced review is what makes the material stick after the course is finished.

| When | What | Time |
|---|---|---:|
| Start of every session | Answer the previous lesson's review questions | 5 min |
| End of every section | Section quiz, flashcards, and recalling the section's key takeaways before reading them | 15-20 min |
| One week after a section | Re-read the section overview and redo its flashcards | 10 min |
| One month after the course | [Course recap](../reference/course-recap.md), [JQL cheat sheet](../reference/jql-cheat-sheet.md), and [Scrum vs Kanban](../reference/scrum-vs-kanban.md) | 20-30 min |

## Staying consistent

- **Fix the time slot.** The same time every study day works better than finding time.
- **Set a minimum.** On busy days one lesson is enough to keep the habit going.
- **Track sessions privately.** Copy [`_templates/study-log.md`](../_templates/study-log.md) to `_personal/study-log.md`, which is git-ignored, and add one `_templates/study-session.md` entry per session.
- **Commit after every session.** The git history becomes a visible record of progress.
- **Space it out.** Stopping at the planned point and coming back the next day beats cramming several sections at once.
