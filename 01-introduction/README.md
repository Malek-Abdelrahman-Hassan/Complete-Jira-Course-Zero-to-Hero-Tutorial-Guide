## Key takeaways

- Jira is Atlassian's work management tool. It is strongest with Agile teams but works for almost any kind of team.
- Jira Software and Jira Work Management are now one product, **Jira**. Jira Service Management (customer requests) stays separate, and Jira Product Discovery (idea validation) is a newer, separate product.
- Projects are now **spaces**, issues are **work items**, and issue types are **work types**. The old words still appear in places, and JQL's `project` keeps working while newer names such as `space` roll out.
- The course targets **Jira Cloud**. Self-managed Jira Data Center sites can look and behave differently, but the concepts transfer. Data Center reaches end of life in March 2029, so Cloud is the long-term version.
- The interface changes constantly, so learn the logic of how things fit together rather than the exact location of buttons.
- A free Jira Cloud site (up to 10 users) is the safest place to practise. It lives at `name.atlassian.net`, and the name can't be changed later.
- Start with a **Scrum** space (company-managed if offered) to get a backlog, a board, and sprints.
- The top bar and sidebar are site-wide (search, Create, settings, profile, spaces, filters, dashboards); each space has its own views such as Board and Backlog.
- The course teaches the user's point of view. At work, admin settings are usually off-limits, so user-level skills matter most.

## Hands-on practice

Suggested exercises for a free Jira Cloud site (these are not part of the official course material):

1. Create a free Jira Cloud site with a Scrum space (company-managed if offered). Name it after the course example, *Stamps and Points*, to match the videos.
2. Explore the main navigation and locate your spaces, filters, dashboards, the settings gear, and your personal settings.
3. Open your personal settings and review the notification, language, and time zone options.
4. Use the search bar to find your space and its board, then switch between **Board** and **Backlog**.
5. Ask Rovo (paid plans and trials only; the Free plan doesn't include it) a question such as "Why is my board empty?" and compare its answer with the lesson.
6. Compare the old and new terms in the [terminology reference](../reference/jira-terminology-changes.md) with what your own site shows.
