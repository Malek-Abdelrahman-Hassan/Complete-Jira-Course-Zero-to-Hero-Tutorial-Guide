## Practice questions

<details>
<summary>1. A colleague mentions "Jira Software" and "Jira Work Management". What are they called today?</summary>
Both are now part of a single product called Jira, which covers software development and general work management.
</details>

<details>
<summary>2. A support team wants customers to submit requests without seeing the team's boards. Which product fits?</summary>
Jira Service Management. Customers submit requests and get updates, while the team works through them in a queue.
</details>

<details>
<summary>3. A product manager wants to collect customer evidence and test hypotheses before anything gets built. Which product is designed for this?</summary>
Jira Product Discovery. Validated ideas can then become work items in Jira.
</details>

<details>
<summary>4. You write the JQL query space = ABC and it fails on your site. What should you write instead?</summary>
project = ABC. Atlassian is adding new JQL names such as space, but they may not work on every site yet. The old word project works everywhere, including in existing filters.
</details>

<details>
<summary>5. Your company's Jira looks quite different from the course and lacks some features. What is a likely reason?</summary>
It may be a customized or long-running Jira Data Center installation on the company's own servers, which can drift away from Jira Cloud. Company customizations and interface experiments can also change how it looks. Data Center reaches end of life in March 2029, so such companies will be moving to Cloud.
</details>

<details>
<summary>6. How many users can a team have and still use Jira Cloud for free?</summary>
Up to 10 users.
</details>

<details>
<summary>7. Why should the site name be chosen carefully when signing up?</summary>
It becomes the site address (name.atlassian.net) and can't be changed later.
</details>

<details>
<summary>8. Which template and space type does the course recommend for the first space?</summary>
A Scrum template, company-managed if that option is offered, so the space has a backlog, a board, and sprints.
</details>

<details>
<summary>9. You open your new Scrum space and the board is empty. Is something wrong?</summary>
No. The board shows the active sprint, and no sprint has started yet. Plan work in the backlog and start a sprint.
</details>

<details>
<summary>10. Where do you change your own account settings, and where does an admin change settings for the whole site?</summary>
Your own settings are in the profile menu. Site-wide settings are behind the settings (gear) icon.
</details>

<details>
<summary>11. What is Rovo, and how can it help while learning Jira?</summary>
Rovo is Atlassian's AI assistant. You can chat with it and ask why a setting is missing or why an action isn't available. It comes with the paid plans and their trials, not with the Free plan.
</details>

## Easy to confuse

| Concept A | Concept B | How to tell them apart |
|---|---|---|
| Jira | Jira Service Management | Jira is for teams planning and doing work. JSM gives customers a way to request help and follow updates without seeing the team's boards. |
| Jira Cloud | Jira Data Center | Cloud is hosted by Atlassian and gets new features first. Data Center runs on company servers, can be customized far away from Cloud, and reaches end of life in March 2029. |
| Site | Space | A site is the whole Jira instance (name.atlassian.net). A space is one container of work inside the site. |
| Jira space | Confluence space | A Jira space holds work items. A Confluence space holds pages. |
| Settings gear | Profile menu | The gear holds site-wide settings for admins. The profile menu holds your own account settings. |
| Work item | Work type | A work item is a single piece of work (ABC-12). A work type is its category (Story, Bug, Epic). |
