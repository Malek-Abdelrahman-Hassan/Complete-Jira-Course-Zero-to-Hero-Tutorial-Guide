# Codestars Certificate Authority (CCA) Certificate

[Course home](../README.md) › [Section 10: Best Practices and Final Challenge](README.md) › Certificate

## TL;DR

Finishing every item in the course unlocks the **Udemy certificate of completion**, which can be downloaded as a PDF or image and shared through its own URL. The course is also eligible for a separate, optional **CCA certificate**, which requires passing an exam outside Udemy. Neither certificate is a formal accreditation, so a certificate works best alongside evidence of practical use, such as the final challenge.

## Key concepts

- **Udemy certificate of completion**: Issued automatically by Udemy when every lecture and quiz in a paid course is marked complete. It shows the learner's name, the course title, the instructors, the course length, the completion date, and a certificate number and URL that anyone can open to confirm it.
- **CCA certificate**: An optional certificate from the Codestars Certificate Authority, available for Codestars courses. It is earned by passing an official exam on the Codestars website; the certificate item at the end of the course explains how to take it.
- **Completion vs accreditation**: Both certificates show that a course or exam was completed. They are not university credits or an Atlassian certification, so they carry the most weight together with skills you can demonstrate.
- **Credential URL**: The public link to a certificate. Professional profiles use it so viewers can check the certificate themselves.

## How to get the Udemy certificate

1. In the course player, open the curriculum and check that every lecture and quiz has a check mark. A single unmarked item keeps the certificate locked.
2. Before downloading, check the name on your Udemy profile, because the certificate uses it.
3. When the course is complete, the **trophy** icon above the course player changes color. Select it, then select **Get Certificate**.
4. On the certificate page, select **Download** and choose PDF or JPG, or copy the page URL to share it.
5. In the Udemy mobile app, open **My learning**, choose the course, open the **More** menu, and select **Certificate of completion**.

If the trophy stays gray after everything is ticked, refresh the page or wait up to an hour; the certificate can take a moment to appear.

## Sharing it

1. On LinkedIn (or a similar profile), add the certificate under **Licenses & certifications**.
2. Use the course title as the name and *Udemy* as the issuing organization.
3. Enter the certificate number (it starts with `UC-`) as the credential ID and the certificate URL as the credential URL.
4. List the skills the course covered, such as *Jira*, *JQL*, *Scrum*, *Kanban*, and *Jira dashboards*, in the profile's skills section.
5. Optionally link a short write-up of the [final challenge](04-final-challenge.md) to show applied work.

## The optional CCA certificate

1. Open the certificate item at the end of the course and follow its instructions to the official exam.
2. Check the current terms before starting, such as any fee and how many attempts are allowed, because they can change.
3. Passing the exam earns the CCA certificate, which is separate from the Udemy one and can be shared the same way.

## Tips and pitfalls

- **Finish every item, not just the videos.** Quizzes and short text items also need a check mark, and they are the usual reason a certificate stays locked.
- **Pair the certificate with evidence.** A certificate says the course was completed; a working board, a saved JQL filter, or a dashboard you built shows the skill.
- **Keep the link, not just the file.** The certificate URL lets others verify it; a PDF on its own cannot be checked.

> [!TIP]
> Keep personal certificate details (URL, number, completion date) in the git-ignored `_personal/` folder rather than in public notes.
