# Bonus Lesson

[Course home](../README.md) › [Section 10: Best Practices and Final Challenge](README.md) › Lesson 6 · 3:07

## TL;DR

The closing lesson congratulates learners on finishing and encourages them to keep applying what they practised. It suggests sharing the completion certificate on a professional profile, with the matching skills listed, and notes that a problem specific to one organization may call for targeted help rather than another course. The rest of the video is course promotion and is not summarized here.

## Key concepts

- **Knowledge vs practical skill**: Finishing the videos is the start. The goal is to use boards, filters, JQL, dashboards, and automation in real work.
- **Certificate plus skills**: A certificate says a course was completed. Listing the specific skills (for example *Jira*, *JQL*, *Agile*, *Scrum*, *Kanban*) on a profile is what makes it useful to others.
- **Right kind of help**: When a challenge is specific to one team or organization, a general course may not solve it. A Jira admin, an internal Agile coach, or targeted consulting often helps more.

## How to wrap up the course

1. Mark every course item complete so the certificate becomes available. See [Codestars Certificate Authority (CCA) Certificate](07-certificate.md).
2. Add the certificate to a professional profile and list the related skills in its skills section.
3. Keep a practice space alive. The [final challenge](04-final-challenge.md) space is a good starting point.
4. Schedule reviews using the [course recap](../reference/course-recap.md) and the review schedule in the [study plan](../00-start-here/study-plan.md).

## Tips and pitfalls

- **Keep practising after the last video.** Skills from a course fade quickly without use. A small weekly task in Jira keeps shortcuts and JQL familiar.
- **List skills you can actually demonstrate.** Endorsements and certificates mean little if the skills are not backed by real practice.
- **Match the help to the problem.** For a workflow or permission problem at work, the fastest path is usually the Jira administrator, not more videos.
